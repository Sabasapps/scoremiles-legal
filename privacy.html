Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 1, 2026 · Last updated: May 1, 2026

ScoreMiles ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Your email address and password when you create an account.
  • Redemption data: Trip names, redemption details, loyalty program information, points used, cash values, and notes you enter into the app.
  • Usage data: How you interact with the app, including features used and actions taken, collected through our service providers.
  • Purchase data: Subscription status and transaction information processed through Apple's App Store and RevenueCat.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, and app version for technical support purposes.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the ScoreMiles app
  • Process and manage your subscription
  • Send transactional emails such as account confirmation and password reset
  • Send marketing communications about ScoreMiles features and updates (you may opt out at any time)
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the product
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. SERVICE PROVIDERS

We use the following third-party services to operate ScoreMiles:

  • Supabase — database and authentication. Your account and redemption data is stored on Supabase servers. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management and purchase validation. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store — payment processing for subscriptions. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Claude API — document scanning to auto-fill redemption data. Images you scan are sent to Anthropic for processing and are not stored beyond the request. Anthropic Privacy Policy

4. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

Your data is stored securely on Supabase's servers. We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and secure authentication. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your redemption data is private to your account and is not shared with other users or third parties except as described in this policy.

5. DATA RETENTION

We retain your account and redemption data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

6. YOUR RIGHTS

As a US-based user, you have the following rights:

  • Access: You can request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You can update your account information within the app.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt-out of marketing: You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • Data portability: You can request an export of your redemption data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@scoremiles.app.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

ScoreMiles is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. CALIFORNIA PRIVACY RIGHTS

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete your personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell your personal information.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, by sending you an email notification. Your continued use of ScoreMiles after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
